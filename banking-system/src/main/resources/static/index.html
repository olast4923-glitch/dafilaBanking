Before you open

  • Savings grows slowly at 0.05% each month. Deposits are allowed, withdrawals are not.
  • Investment pays 5% monthly and needs at least BWP 500.00 to open. You can deposit and withdraw freely.
  • Cheque is for salary credits. Only working customers can open one, and employer details are required.
  • You may hold any mix of the three account types at the same time.
